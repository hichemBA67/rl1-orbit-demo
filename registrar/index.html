03 · Registrar

What “registrar” means, legally and technically

The registrar keeps the legal record of who owns the bond. On RL1 that record is the smart-contract state, but the legal effect of that state comes from national law (ERIR, DEEP, eWpG, CSD) or from contract. This module takes the role apart layer by layer, then lets you operate a live register.
L4
Legal layer: the law that gives the record its effect

Ley 6/2023 Art. 8 (ERIR) · CMF L.211-7 (DEEP) · eWpG §16–§17 (crypto-securities register) · Depotgesetz + CSDR (OeKB global note) · contract (NL/GB). It decides whether an entry is title, evidence of title, or only a mirror.

L3
Registrar: the accountable legal person

Licensed or designated entity that keeps the register, answers for its accuracy, runs corrections, applies court orders and keeps the off-chain identity mapping. It holds REGISTRAR_ROLE keys in HSM/MPC custody with an m-of-n quorum.

L2
Smart-contract state: the register itself

mapping(address ⇒ balance), frozen, encumbered, totalSupply, termsHash, ISIN. Every transfer updates it atomically. For ES/FR/DE this state is the legal register. For AT it is the CSD's book-entry layer behind the global note.

L1
RL1 ledger: replication & finality

Permissioned EVM network run by the RL1 SCE. Validators order transactions and BFT consensus makes a block final. RL1 does not decide ownership; it guarantees that all nodes hold the same state.

L0
Off-chain: identity & golden copy

KYC files, legal names, LEIs and the address ↔ holder mapping (GDPR: kept off-chain). A golden-copy export is reconciled against the chain each day, which is the contingency required for example by RD 814/2023.

What the registrar does
  • Initial entry: records the issue (issue volume, nominal, ISIN, T&Cs hash) and mints exactly the issue amount, which is the integrity-of-issue principle
  • Eligibility of holders: only identities with valid claims can hold. The registrar (or delegated custodians) issues the claims
  • Transfers: processes holder-signed transfers and venue settlement, rejecting any that break the T&Cs (Reg S, denomination, KYC)
  • Encumbrances: records pledges and freezes (court orders, sanctions, insolvency administrator requests)
  • Corrections: forced transfer after due process: lost keys, error correction, inheritance, resolution action (bail-in)
  • Corporate actions: record-date snapshot for the paying agent; redemption and burn
  • Reporting: register extracts to holders and issuer; CNMV (ES) and BaFin (DE) publications; reconciliation with the golden copy
  • Continuity: contingency plan and migration capability (to a new registrar or a new network)
What the registrar cannot do (by design)
  • Mint beyond the issue amount: totalSupply ≤ issueAmount is enforced in the contract, so increases need an ISSUER_ROLE tap with new Final Terms
  • Move tokens without a logged legal reference: forcedTransfer(from, to, amt, bytes32 legalRef) requires a 3-of-5 quorum and emits an event
  • Settle trades: only Seturion's SETTLEMENT_ROLE can run DvP for venue trades
  • See personal data on-chain: there is none; the mapping lives in the registrar's own systems
  • Rewrite history: RL1 finality makes every past state auditable
