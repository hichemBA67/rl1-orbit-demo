09 · Chartered Investment

ORBIT Special Fund: one unit, the whole RL1 basket

Chartered Investment, the registrar of the RL1 tranches, initiates a German Spezial-AIF that buys the ORBIT bonds in the primary books and on Seturion. Its units are electronic fund units on RL1, and Chartered Investment keeps the unit register. A pension fund that buys one unit gets diversified exposure to all twelve issuers, with T+0 settlement on both sides.
