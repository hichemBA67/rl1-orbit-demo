05 · OeKB CSD & Eurosystem

OeKB’s “rubber stamp” on every tranche

Since 30 March 2026 the Eurosystem accepts DLT-based marketable assets issued through CSD-operated DLT services, provided they meet every standard criterion and sit in an eligible SSS reachable via T2S (ECB, 27 Jan 2026). Assets that exist only on DLT, outside eligible SSSs, are still under study. OeKB CSD is issuer CSD for all 12 tranches, so every bond passes the CSD criterion. That is necessary but not enough: a tranche must also be in EUR with an EEA issuer and governing law. Today that means Erste and RBI. Switch any EEA tranche to EUR on the Primary page before pricing and it becomes eligible too.
1
Issuer CSD for all 12

Every issuer, from ABN AMRO to Standard Chartered, chooses OeKB CSD as issuer CSD (CSDR Art. 49: freedom to choose any EU CSD; the issuer's home corporate law still applies). OeKB performs the notary and central-maintenance services for every issue.

2
Global note or collective entry

Austrian-law tranches: signed Sammelurkunde in collective custody (Depotgesetz). Other tranches: the home-law register (eWpG collective entry, DEEP, ERIR or contractual register) kept by the registrar records OeKB CSD as holder of the whole issue. Investors hold through OeKB book entries.

3
CSD-operated DLT service

OeKB deploys and operates the BondToken on RL1 under CSD_OPERATOR_ROLE. Token balances are OeKB's book entries. Transfers settled by Seturion run through OeKB's contract and are CSD book-entry movements.

4
ISIN as national numbering agency

OeKB allocates AT0000ORB… ISINs to all 12 tranches (illustrative). Issue in an EEA CSD satisfies the place-of-issue criterion.

5
Integrity of issue

Intraday reconciliation: Σ token balances = global-note amount (CSDR Art. 37). A mismatch pauses the contract.

6
T2S reachability

Positions can be realigned to Eurosystem collateral accounts via T2S. ECMS sees the asset like any other OeKB-issued bond.

7
Eligibility submission

OeKB/the issuer submits the eligibility request to OeNB. After assessment the ISINs are added to the Eligible Assets Database.
