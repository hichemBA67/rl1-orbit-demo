RL1 ORBIT: 12 issuers, six legal systems, one ledger

Cecabank coordinates a joint DLT bond programme on RL1. The eight RL1 banks (ABN AMRO, Crédit Mutuel Alliance Fédérale, DekaBank, LBBW, Natixis, DZ BANK, NatWest and Standard Chartered) each issue the same plain vanilla bond: USD 100m, fixed coupon, 3-year bullet, no options. Each bond is issued under the bank's home law, and Chartered Investment keeps the register. CaixaBank and CACEIS issue alongside them; Erste and Raiffeisen issue EUR 100m. OeKB CSD rubber-stamps every tranche: it is issuer CSD for all 12, holds the global positions, allocates AT ISINs and runs a CSD-operated DLT service. There is no exchange listing. All trading and settlement runs on the Seturion DLT TSS. Chartered Investment also runs an ORBIT special fund that buys a basket of the bonds.

What OeKB's stamp does, and doesn't, do for ECB eligibility
With OeKB as issuer CSD, every tranche meets the CSD criterion: issued in a CSD-operated DLT service inside an eligible, T2S-connected SSS. Eligibility still needs a euro denomination and an EEA issuer and governing law. Right now 2 of 12 tranches are eligible. The USD tranches fail on currency, and NatWest and Standard Chartered fail on issuer and law. Since nothing is listed on an exchange, eligibility also assumes the Eurosystem accepts Seturion as an acceptable market.
